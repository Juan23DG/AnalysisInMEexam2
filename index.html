Core Learning Workflow

🔄 How the 3-Stage Step-by-Step Tutoring Process Works

Every problem on the site (in both the Interactive Tutor and the Mock Exam Carousel) walks you through three structured stages:

1 Identify the Solution Style

On an exam, the hardest step is often deciding which method to use. Stage 1 presents the ODE and asks you to choose the right solution strategy via multiple-choice.

  • Immediate Diagnostic Feedback: Explains why your choice works—or why a tempting alternative fails (e.g., variable vs. constant coefficients, resonance overlap).
  • 🙈 Blind Mode Tip: Enable Blind Mode in the sidebar to hide topic labels until you solve Stage 1!
2 Governing Equations & Recipe

Once Stage 1 is solved (or if you click Skip to Equations), the tutor unlocks the exact formula sheet equations and a step-by-step roadmap for that method.

  • No Spoiler Algebra Yet: Gives you the integrating factor $\mu(x)$, Wronskian $W$, or Laplace shift rule so you can solve the ODE yourself first.
  • 📘 Outside Lecture Notes Button: Opens a slide-over drawer with the exact classroom board pages for that problem.
3 Solve, Compare Checkpoints & Verify

Work out the problem on paper or on the ✏️ Scratchpad, then check your work at your own pace:

  • 💡 Checkpoints 1 & 2: Peek at the First Step Setup or Intermediate Integral / Algebra Check anytime without revealing the full solution.
  • Smart 2-Attempt Answer Check: If your 1st attempt is wrong, Checkpoints 1 & 2 unlock automatically so you can fix your algebra and try a 2nd time!

📂 Explore All 6 Exam 2 Topics (Click Any Topic to Practice)

Topic 1 • Separable & Linear FO 20 Qs

4 HW3 + 16 Bank: Separation of variables $g(y)dy = f(x)dx$, standard linear form $y' + P(x)y = f(x)$, integrating factor $\mu(x) = e^{\int P(x)dx}$, IVPs & intervals of validity.

Topic 2 • Bernoulli & Substitutions 20 Qs

4 HW3 + 16 Bank: Bernoulli ODEs $y' + P(x)y = f(x)y^n$ via $v = y^{1-n}$, homogeneous ratio ODEs via $v = y/x$ ($dy = v\,dx + x\,dv$), and linear shifts $u = ax + by + c$.

Topic 3 • Higher-Order & Cauchy-Euler 20 Qs

5 HW3 + 15 Bank: Auxiliary equations (distinct, repeated, complex roots), 3rd/4th-order ODEs, Reduction of Order $y_2 = y_1\int \frac{e^{-\int P\,dx}}{y_1^2}dx$, and Cauchy-Euler $ax^2y'' + bxy' + cy = 0$.

Topic 4 • Undetermined Coeffs & VoP 20 Qs

5 HW4 + 15 Bank: Superposition trial solutions $y_p$, the $x^s$ resonance multiplication rule, and Variation of Parameters $u_1' = -y_2 g/W,\; u_2' = y_1 g/W$ (including $\sec x, \tan x, \csc 2x$).

Topic 5 • Direct & Inverse Laplace 20 Qs

4 HW4 + 16 Bank: First Shifting Theorem ($e^{at}$), Second Shifting Theorem ($u(t-c)$), frequency differentiation ($t^n f(t)$), Gamma function, completing the square, partial fractions & convolution.

Topic 6 • Laplace IVPs & Mechanics 20 Qs

4 HW4 + 16 Bank: Constant-coefficient & delayed-step IVPs, Dirac delta impulses $\delta(t-c)$, spring-mass-damper resonance & shock absorbers, RLC circuits, Volterra integral equations & beams $EI w^{(4)}$.
