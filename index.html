1. Separable & Linear 1st-Order 20 Qs

What's inside: Separating $y$ and $x$, integrating factor $\mu(x) = e^{\int P(x)dx}$, and intervals of validity.

👀 Spot it: 1st-order ($y'$) that either factors as $g(x)h(y)$ or is linear in $y$.

2. Bernoulli & Substitutions 20 Qs

What's inside: Bernoulli ($u = y^{1-n}$), ratio substitution ($v = y/x$), and linear shifts ($u = ax+by+c$).

👀 Spot it: Nonlinear 1st-order with an extra $y^n$ power, $y/x$ fractions, or $(x+y)$ inside a function.

3. Higher-Order & Cauchy-Euler 20 Qs

What's inside: Characteristic roots ($e^{rx}$), Reduction of Order ($y_2 = u y_1$), and Cauchy-Euler ($x^2 y'' + bxy' + cy = 0$).

👀 Spot it: Right-hand side equals $0$ ($y'', y''', y^{(4)}$), or a known solution $y_1(x)$ is given.

4. Undetermined Coeffs & VoP 20 Qs

What's inside: Guessing $y_p$ (with $x^s$ resonance fix) and Variation of Parameters ($W, u_1', u_2'$).

👀 Spot it: Non-zero right-hand side $g(x)$! Use UC for poly/$e^{ax}$/sin/cos; use VoP for $\sec, \tan, \ln$, or fractions.

5. Direct & Inverse Laplace 20 Qs

What's inside: 1st Shift ($e^{at}$), 2nd Shift ($u(t-c)$), partial fractions, completing the square & Gamma function.

👀 Spot it: Asks for $\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\}$ or $\mathcal{L}^{-1}\{F(s)\}$ directly (no differential equation yet).

6. Laplace IVPs & Mechanics 20 Qs

What's inside: Solving IVPs with initial conditions, step switches $u(t-c)$, springs, RLC circuits & beams.

👀 Spot it: An IVP with initial conditions at $t=0$ or a spring/RLC/beam mechanics model.

7. Heaviside & Dirac Delta Forcing 20 Qs • NEW

What's inside: Setting up piecewise forcing with $u(t-c)$, applying 2nd Shift ($e^{-cs}F(s)$), and impulse forcing $\delta(t-c)$.

👀 Spot it: Piecewise braces, delayed ramps/waves, rectangular pulses, or hammer blows $\delta(t-c)$.
